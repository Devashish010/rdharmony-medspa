IV Iron Infusion
Therapy

Replenish critical iron stores directly through intravenous therapy. Faster absorption, lasting results, administered by our licensed medical professionals in the comfort of your home.

Why IV Therapy?

The Benefits of IV Iron Infusion

⚡

Fast Acting

Intravenous delivery bypasses the digestive system, providing significantly faster iron absorption than oral supplements.

From $180 per session
🧬

Medical Grade

Hospital-quality iron formulations administered using sterile technique by our licensed registered nurses and practitioners.

From $180 per session
🏠

At-Home Comfort

No hospital waiting rooms. Receive your infusion in the comfort of your own home, office, or preferred location.

From $180 per session
📊

Lab Monitored

We review your blood work and iron studies to create a treatment plan precisely calibrated to your needs.

💊

No Side Effects

Unlike oral iron which causes GI discomfort, IV infusions bypass the gut entirely — no constipation or upset stomach.

🔄

Maintenance Plans

Customized maintenance schedules to keep your iron levels optimal and energy high, year-round.

Who Is IV Iron Therapy For?

IV iron infusion therapy is ideal for individuals with diagnosed iron deficiency anaemia, those who cannot tolerate oral iron, patients with inflammatory bowel disease, pregnant women with severe iron deficiency, and anyone seeking faster iron repletion than oral supplements can provide. A physician referral or recent bloodwork is recommended before your first session.
